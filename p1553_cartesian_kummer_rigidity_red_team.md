## Accepted rigidity theorem

Let `psi:P^1->P^1` be nonconstant of degree `d_psi`, put
`f=psi o x`, and assume `2 d_psi<N`. For distinct Kummer classes represented
by `A'!=+-A`, compare the complete unordered branch divisors

```text
B_A(Q)=[f(A+Q)]+[f(A-Q)].
```

The four translated functions have at most `8 d_psi` combined pole points.
Away from them, each nonzero trace or norm difference has at most
`8 d_psi` zeros. More than that many regular branch equalities make both
differences identically zero.

Unique factorization of the two monic branch polynomials matches their two
linear roots. The unswapped match makes `f` invariant under `A-A'`. In the
swapped match, evenness gives

```text
f(X)=f(A+A'-X)=f(X-(A+A')).
```

The resulting nonzero translation has order `N`. Invariance factors `f`
through a degree-`N` quotient, so `N` divides `deg(f)=2 d_psi`, a
contradiction. Complete branch equality therefore occurs at no more than
`16 d_psi` second-list points, or `8 d_psi` on a pole-free deck. A full
Cartesian deck of size `B>16 d_psi` admits no nontrivial branch-oblivious
merge.

## Accepted support correction

```text

```

Each colliding source pair has at most `16 d_psi` compatible second inputs.
Thus the general pair-collision/second-input count is bounded by

```text
8 d_psi(d_psi-1)B = O(d_psi^2 B).
```

Retaining `Theta(B^2)` genuinely compressed occurrences requires
`d_psi=Omega(B^(1/2))`. Only an indivisible whole-fiber state, with no
input-dependent subdivision or correction, gets the stronger `O(d_psi B)`
bound and `d_psi=Omega(B)` requirement.

These are support bounds, not relation-density or independent-rank theorems.
