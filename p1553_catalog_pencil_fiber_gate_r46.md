## Exact pencil test

Each retained block is a nine-element subset of offsets `0,...,80` with sum
360. Its normalized section `s_D` in `H^0(E,O(9O))` vanishes exactly at those
nine translated subgroup points. R46 recomputes every section and verifies its
zero set on the full order-103 subgroup. The 2,529 block instances deduplicate
to

```text
807 distinct blocks = 807 distinct projective sections. (1)
```

For two sections `s_0,s_1` with disjoint zero divisors, the rational map

```text
f(P)=(s_0(P):s_1(P))                            (2)
```

is basepoint-free on the subgroup. Equal projective values are pencil fibers.
R46 evaluates (2) on all 103 subgroup points and asserts that no subgroup fiber
has more than nine points, then measures multiplicities on the selected
81-point interval.

Two section pairs span the same pencil exactly when their normalized 36
Plucker coordinates agree. This gives exact projective deduplication before
fiber scoring.
