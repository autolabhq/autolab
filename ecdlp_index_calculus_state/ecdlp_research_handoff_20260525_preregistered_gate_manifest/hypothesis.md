# Hypothesis

The root-hyperplane selector's strongest 80+ evidence should be converted from
an ex post diagnostic gate into a preregistered materialization gate.

The proposed pre-factor gate is:

- the full materialized FFE surface has at least one selected root pair
- the selected public leaf has at least one known hit root before Sage
  factorization

This gate does not inspect post-factor fields such as `public_zero_root_count`,
chosen root policy, `below_rho`, or direct-root cost.  If it isolates the same
non-vacuous public-zero-capable surfaces on the existing controls, it becomes a
usable rule to freeze before evaluating a fresh 80+ bank.
